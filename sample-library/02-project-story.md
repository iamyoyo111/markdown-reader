# Project Story

## A difficult delivery

**Situation:** A hospital needed to replace its electrical distribution system while the building remained fully operational.

**Task:** I coordinated the shutdown plan across the client, facility teams, contractors, and design consultants.

**Action:** I divided the work into short, reversible phases. Each phase included a risk review, a rollback point, and a communication checklist.

**Result:** We completed the changeover without interrupting critical clinical services and reduced unplanned downtime by 35 percent.
